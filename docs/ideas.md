# Ideas Backlog

## Purpose

This file stores raw and structured ideas so they are captured outside memory and can be refined later.

## Capture Format

When adding a new idea, use:

- **Title**
- **Problem**
- **Proposed Approach**
- **Impact**
- **Next Step**

---

## Idea 1: Vehicle-Integrated Exoskeleton Charging Dock

- **Problem:** Users must unstrap from the suit to plug in, reducing convenience and increasing friction.
- **Proposed Approach:** Convert vehicle seating into an integrated dock with either inductive charging surfaces or self-aligning smart couplers.
- **Impact:** Seamless recharge during transit and better user adoption.
- **Next Step:** Draft electrical interface requirements and safety constraints.

## Idea 2: Motion-Harvested Supplementary Charging

- **Problem:** Kinetic energy from driving is wasted as heat/noise.
- **Proposed Approach:**
  - Regenerative suspension dampers (vehicle-side generation)
  - Micro-vibration harvesters in suit frame (piezo/magnetostrictive)
- **Impact:** Supplemental trickle charging and improved energy efficiency.
- **Next Step:** Estimate realistic watt recovery per hour under typical road conditions.

## Idea 3: Seat-Suit Structural & Safety Coupling

- **Problem:** Rigid frame discomfort and unclear crash integration while seated.
- **Proposed Approach:** Design the seat as a structural mating interface for support plus optional crash-lock behavior.
- **Impact:** Better ergonomics and potentially enhanced occupant protection.
- **Next Step:** Define docking geometry and emergency release logic.

## Idea 4: Adaptive Prompting from Biometric State

- **Problem:** AI interaction quality may degrade if user physiological state is not considered.
- **Proposed Approach:** Use real-time telemetry state to tune prompt tone, pacing, and response strategy.
- **Impact:** Improved human-system interaction and safety-aware assistance.
- **Next Step:** Create state-to-prompt adaptation matrix.
